How we work

Brand & identity

The mark, the wordmark, the palette, the words we lead with, and the rules that keep them consistent. Every asset is generated deterministically from one vector source, and a gate rejects a change where the generators and the committed art disagree.

The words we lead with

These are the approved forms. They exist because a product describes itself the same way everywhere or it does not have a description at all.

UseText
TaglineUntangle the work — yours, and your agents'.
Product descriptionSpagitty, your gateway to a Git-managed agent farm.

Short descriptorA desktop Git client built for the review that follows fast work.
CategoryDesktop Git client — gateway to a Git-managed agent farm.

Rules the wording has to keep

  • Never “AI-powered”. Nothing in Spagitty is a model. Saying otherwise trades away the one thing a local-first tool has, which is that you can tell what it is doing.
  • The possessive is on the agents, not the user. “yours, and your agents'” — plural possessive, apostrophe after the s. It is the most commonly mistyped part of the tagline.
  • The em dash is an em dash, not a hyphen, and the tagline ends in a full stop. It is a sentence.
  • Keep the metaphor literal. Tangled and straightened, pasta and strands. Do not extend it into cooking, chefs or kitchens in product copy — that register belongs to the delight layer, where it is deliberately a joke, and mixing the two makes the serious claims read as jokes too.

The name and the metaphor

Spagitty — spa-gi-ty — is a deliberate portmanteau of spaghetti and Git. A complex repository without clear lane graphs, divergence tracking and structured review trails quickly becomes a messy plate of tangled pasta. The mission is to untangle the pasta bowl into clear, straight strands of intent.

Tone of voice

  • Honest and direct. We state what actions do, without patronising marketing speak or artificial excitement.
  • Crafted and precise. Geometry is deliberate, flat, and legible down to a 16-pixel icon.
  • Respectful of the machine. Zero bloat, instant response, local-first offline operation.

The mark

  • The amber plate (#EEB04D) — a warm rounded ground, the plate the strands lie on.
  • Four dark strands (#454447) — beginning tangled and overlapping at the top and resolving into straight, parallel paths flowing downward.
  • The master source is assets/brand/mark.svg on a 912 × 953 viewBox.
Flat, and non-destructive

The mark contains zero gradients, drop shadows, inner bevels or outlines. And the vector path data is permanent: it is never manually redrawn or replaced by an algorithmic approximation.

Clearspace

A mandatory boundary of 1×, where × is 15% of the mark's total height, surrounds the mark and the lockup on all four sides. No text, icon, border or secondary element may intrude into it.

The wordmark and the lockup

Lowercase spagitty, set in Inter at weight 660 with +0.045em tracking. Strictly lowercase.

The lockup puts the mark on the left and the wordmark on the right along a shared optical centreline, with a gap of about 20% of the mark's height. There are two ink variants — light text for dark backgrounds, dark charcoal for light ones — because a single lockup cannot be legible on both.

Determinism is a gate

Gate 2 in CI runs both generators in --check mode and rejects a change whose asset bytes have drifted. Regenerate collateral with the scripts; never hand-edit a derived PNG.

Binding rules

  1. Never use the Git logo or Git orange (#F05133). Spagitty is an independent client and is not affiliated with the Git project.
  2. Never redraw or approximate the mark. No procedural noodles, no bezier approximations, no CSS pseudo-element icons. Reference the source or a generated asset.
  3. Never add gradients or drop shadows to brand assets. All branding is strictly flat.
  4. Always use theme-aware CSS variables — var(--accent), var(--brand-amber) — never a raw hex in a component style where light-mode adaptability is required.
  5. Enforce deterministic generation. Run the generators whenever collateral changes.
  6. Preserve the menu-bar template rules. macOS menu-bar and tray icons carry the four strands and no plate.
